## Software & data

**SMBH Spin–Mass Compilation**

[Github repository](https://github.com/jsiskreynes-astro/smbh-mass-spin-compilation/tree/main)

A continuously updated compilation of supermassive black-hole mass and spin measurements derived from X-ray reflection spectroscopy.

This compilation was developed in association with the [Sisk-Reynes et al., 2026, Galaxies, 14, 3, id.50](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4434/14/3/50).

**Tools for analyzing _Chandra_ X-ray observations of Lensed Quasars**  

Python, bash, and CIAO_embedded scripts for analyzing _Chandra_ X-ray observations of strongly lensed quasars to conduct milliarcsecond X-ray--to--optical astrometry.

This repository is currently in progress and includes:

- _Chandra_ data extraction scripts,
-  _Chandra_ ray-tracing modules for generating realistic simulated _Chandra_ observations at the same positions as the optical lensed images,
-  Tools for source-plane X-ray contour reconstruction  around the source-plane optical emission region for an array of putative solutions with pre-computed maximum likelihood values,
-  Demonstration notebooks showcasing the application of the above tools to highly-magnified lensed quasars with publicly accessible X-ray and optical datasets.

These tools form the backbone of my current research program on sub-kpc AGN structure and emission-region offsets.
